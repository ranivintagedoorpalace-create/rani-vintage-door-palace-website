About Us

Where Every Grain Holds a Royal Legacy.

Rani Vintage Door Palace is a traditional Chettinad door makers hub from Karaikudi. We specialize in antique temple doors, sourced from aged solid teak wood and carved entirely by hand — no MDF, no duplicate wood, no shortcuts.

From a single main door to a grand palace entrance, each door is built to order, carved with divine stories, sized to your home, and built to last for generations.
